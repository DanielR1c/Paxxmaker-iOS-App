# PaxxMaker



PaxxMaker – your 3D prints, always under control.


The ultimate control app for your Snapmaker U1 with paxx12 custom firmware and Klipper-based single-nozzle printers. Everything in view – from iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch.

By default everything runs locally on your network – cloud features only if you want them.

## Control & monitor

- Live print progress with percentage and ETA
- Real-time temperatures: extruder, heated bed, motors, chamber
- Pause, resume, stop & emergency stop
- Browse files and start prints directly
- Live camera stream

## Notifications

- Push on print finished, cancelled, paused and empty filament – on the U1 with the affected nozzle
- Server push works even with the app closed; local mode stays entirely on your own network
- Live Activity on the Lock Screen and Dynamic Island

## Smart plug

- Switch Tuya and Shelly plugs and see live wattage
- Auto Shutdown: the plug switches off automatically after a print – with an adjustable delay, optionally after a cancel too
- Cost per print: power consumption plus filament used, priced via your Spoolman spool or the slicer profile – with history and per-head details

## Spoolman – filament tracked automatically

- Keep track of how full each spool is
- Change spools right from the app
- Per-nozzle tracking – assign a spool to each nozzle, and in multi-colour prints Spoolman deducts from the right spool the moment the nozzle changes
- SpoolLink: the assignment follows the spool's RFID tag – even when moved to another nozzle

## PaxxMaker Slicer

- Open the STL on your phone, place it on the build plate, scale it and paint faces for the individual heads. [PaxxMaker-Connect](https://github.com/DanielR1c/PaxxMaker-Connect) builds the bridge between the app and the slicer workflow.
- Thingiverse is now built into the app: browse models and send them straight to your print bed with one tap.

## Multiple printers

- Swipe between your printers
- Own colour theme per printer
- Automatic printer discovery on the network
- Remote access via OctoEverywhere

## Widgets & Apple Watch

- Home Screen widgets with live progress
- Apple Watch app with real-time data and complications

## NFC spools

- Read and write filament spools via NFC – colour, material and temperature range right on the tag

## Make it yours

- Arrange and hide tiles freely
- Custom G-code macros with icons and colours
- Firmware configuration and calibration right from the app
- 7 languages: German, English, French, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese, Chinese (Simplified)
